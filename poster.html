Tuesday 24 November 2026
The Lead · Shenzhen, China

Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen

The leaders of the United States, China and Russia held a landmark trilateral summit in Shenzhen, the first meeting of its kind, overshadowing the APEC gathering and raising hopes and fears over trade and the war in Ukraine.

Ukraine · Libya · Poland

Ukraine says sea drones sank a Russian warship in Libya

Kyiv says its naval drones destroyed the corvette Mercury in Benghazi harbour, over 1,000 miles from home. Overnight, Russian drone strikes on the Kyiv region killed 11 people, and a Russian drone entered Polish airspace, putting Poland’s air defence on alert.

Business · Technology

Nvidia reinstates CEO Jensen Huang

Nvidia’s board restored Huang as chief executive 11 days after he resigned, following intervention by President Trump. Washington blacklisted Singapore chip firm Megaspeed over alleged smuggling, and Nvidia shares surged.

Asia

Russian jets trigger shelter alert in South Korea

Russian warplanes entered South Korea’s air defence zone without notice; residents of Ulleung Island were told to take shelter as Seoul scrambled jets.

Middle East

Tensions flare between Iran and the United States

Iran’s Revolutionary Guard said three tankers struck sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz. The US sanctioned Iranian money-exchange networks, and Trump warned of a ‘very traumatic’ outcome if nuclear talks fail.

Climate

COP31 climate talks under way in Antalya

World leaders in Türkiye are debating climate finance and fossil fuel commitments, with deep divisions between rich and developing nations.
