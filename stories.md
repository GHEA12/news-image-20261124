# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

The stories on the front page (entry.png), as researched and cross-checked on
24 November 2026, with the reporting behind each item.

## Lead story

**Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — The leaders of the United States,
China and Russia held a landmark trilateral summit in Shenzhen, the first
meeting of its kind, overshadowing the APEC gathering and raising hopes and
fears over trade and the war in Ukraine.
*Source note: summit coverage led the day's wires, including Reuters.*

## Story 2

**Ukraine says sea drones sank a Russian warship in Libya** — Kyiv says its
naval drones destroyed the corvette Mercury in Benghazi harbour, over 1,000
miles from home. Overnight, Russian drone strikes on the Kyiv region killed
11 people, and a Russian drone entered Polish airspace, putting Poland's air
defence on alert.
*Source note: reported by The Guardian, the BBC and wire services.*

## Story 3

**Nvidia reinstates CEO Jensen Huang** — Nvidia's board restored Huang as
chief executive 11 days after he resigned, following intervention by
President Trump. Washington blacklisted Singapore chip firm Megaspeed over
alleged smuggling, and Nvidia shares surged.
*Source note: reported by the BBC and The Guardian's business coverage.*

## Story 4

**Tensions flare between Iran and the United States** — Iran's Revolutionary
Guard said three tankers struck sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz. The US
sanctioned Iranian money-exchange networks, and Trump warned of a 'very
traumatic' outcome if nuclear talks fail.
*Source note: reported by Al Jazeera and Reuters.*

## Story 5

**Russian jets trigger shelter alert in South Korea** — Russian warplanes
entered South Korea's air defence zone without notice; residents of Ulleung
Island were told to take shelter as Seoul scrambled jets.
*Source note: reported by The Guardian and Deutsche Welle.*

## Story 6

**COP31 climate talks under way in Antalya** — World leaders in Türkiye are
debating climate finance and fossil fuel commitments, with deep divisions
between rich and developing nations.
*Source note: reported by Deutsche Welle and Al Jazeera.*
